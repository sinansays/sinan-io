About

At my core, I celebrate the hacker ethos: curiosity, craftsmanship, and a love for tinkering and clever solutions, always with a dash of humility and humor. I draw inspiration from visionaries like Douglas Engelbart, JCR Licklider, Bill Atkinson, Jef Raskin, Alan Kay, Bret Victor, and Andy Matuschak.

What I Do

I’m the founder of GeneralCognition (GenCog), an AI advisory practice that helps organizations adopt AI responsibly and effectively: spanning governance design, workforce training and enablement, build-vs-buy vendor evaluation, use-case prioritization, and implementation strategy. Current engagements include enterprise AI governance and implementation for a global aviation asset management firm, with work across U.S. and European offices.

I also developed and teach "AI for Product Management" at UCLA, now in its 7th quarter with 150% QoQ registration growth, translating the same practitioner-level understanding of how AI systems actually work into a curriculum that working professionals can apply immediately.

Background

Technical product management across enterprise SaaS, health-tech/diagnostics, and IoT — where I translated AI/ML capabilities into trustworthy, human-centered products. Selected outcomes:

  • At Revenue.io, shipped LLM-powered GenAI features (‘Ask Revenue’ chatbot, Conversation Highlights, GenAI Scorecards, etc.) that produced 5x engagement lift at launch.
  • At LynxDx, hired and led an 8-person eng/IT/systems team to scale-up lab ops software that won us public-sector contracts exceeding $100M in the highly regulated healthcare/diagnostics environment (HIPAA/CAP/CLIA/NIST).
  • At Dealpath, supported >$10T in transactions evaluated on-platform by shipping 10+ workflow features (some +250% MoM growth) with cited customer impact of 5+ hours/week saved/person, >$1B+ closed/committed (and $8.5B closed for a marquee client in 2021).

Interdisciplinary training in informatics/HCI and law/policy — which turns out to be exactly what AI adoption work demands.
