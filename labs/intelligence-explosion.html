Software Feedback Loops toward Recursive AI R&D: Assessing and Planning for an Intelligence Explosion

Presented by Michael Sinanian · Wed Oct 7, 2026 · on arXiv:2609.36054

Frontier labs now publish their own numbers on how much of their AI research is already done by AI. This paper argues that a software-only feedback loop, where better AI expands the research workforce that builds better AI, could compound into an intelligence explosion, and that even a meaningful chance of that justifies preparing now. The talk traces the idea’s 60-year lineage, the paper’s evidence and the frictions that could stop the loop, then its policy proposals.
