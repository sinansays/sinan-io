Inspired by the satirical premise of Terry Bisson’s “They’re Made Out of Meat,” with a different object of extraterrestrial bewilderment: not that humans are made of meat, but that they cannot seem to encounter anything, even the prospect of transcending their own intelligence, without finding a way to monetize it.
