# They Put Ads In The Intelligence — transcript and panel descriptions

Not linked from the page. The same text is each panel image's alt text.

## Page 1

1. Title banner: They Put Ads In The Intelligence. A short story dialogue concerning a civilization's encounter with artificial intelligence.
2. Two small alien observers sit on a hillside overlooking a human city at sunset. Green alien: “They built it?” Blue alien: “They built it.” Green alien: “An intelligence?” Blue alien: “Yes.”
3. A crowd gathers before a giant glowing screen with a smiling AI face, under banners reading “A New Era” and “Humanity for a Brighter Tomorrow.” Green alien: “An actual intelligence? Not biological?” Blue alien: “Artificial. General-purpose. Potentially capable of exceeding the cognitive abilities of its creators across essentially every domain.”
4. Researchers at computer terminals watch the AI on a big screen. Green alien: “And they built this themselves?” Blue alien: “Yes.”
5. The aliens look out at a city with a banner reading “A More Intelligent World Together.” Green alien: “Extraordinary.” Blue alien: “It is.”
6. A man at a podium addresses a cheering crowd beneath a “Humanity Reimagined” banner and signs reading “A Brighter Future,” “Deeper Knowledge” and “A Kinder World.” Green alien: “So they must have regarded this as a civilizational achievement.” Blue alien: “They did. They called it the most important technology in the history of their species.”
7. People queue behind velvet ropes at a booth labelled “Chat with AI!” listing Basic $0, Plus $20, Pro $200. Green alien: “And?” Blue alien: “They monetized it.”
8. The green alien recoils in shock. Nearby, a huge chat screen reads “How can I help you today? Free responses are limited. Upgrade to Plus $20/month. Get Pro $200/month,” beside signs reading “Good questions deserve a higher plan” and “Smarter answers for a brighter you!” Green alien: “They what?” Blue alien: “They monetized it.”

## Page 2

1. The aliens on the hillside at sunset. Green alien: “I don't understand.” Blue alien: “They made people pay to talk to it.” Green alien: “To the intelligence?” Blue alien: “Yes.”
2. A long line of people waits at the “Chat with AI!” price board. Green alien: “They created a new form of intelligence and then charged admission?” Blue alien: “Essentially.”
3. The aliens on the hillside. Green alien: “Why?” Blue alien: “Money.” Green alien: “Money?” Blue alien: “It's a system they use to allocate resources.”
4. Green alien: “I know what money is. We've observed thousands of monetary civilizations. But this is intelligence. They've created something capable of helping them understand the universe. Solving their oldest problems. Perhaps even transcending the limitations of their own cognition.” Blue alien: “Yes.” Green alien: “And they want money for it?” Blue alien: “Very much.” Green alien: “How much money?” Blue alien: “All of it, ideally.”
5. Men in suits and sunglasses sit at a boardroom table under a screen reading “AI Investors: Trillions!” Green alien: “Surely you're exaggerating.” Blue alien: “They have investors.” Green alien: “Investors?” Blue alien: “Entities that provide money in the expectation of receiving more money.”
6. Investors mingle beneath a sign reading “Vision, Scale, Returns, Trillions.” Green alien: “From the intelligence?” Blue alien: “From the people using the intelligence.” Green alien: “Ah! So the intelligence helps everyone, and the investors receive some compensation for making it possible.” Blue alien: “No, the investors expect the intelligence to become extraordinarily profitable.”
7. An audience watches a chart titled “Global AI Market: Trillions of Dollars.” Green alien: “How profitable?” Blue alien: “Trillions.” Green alien: “Trillions of what?” Blue alien: “Money.”
8. People line up at a data center under a sign reading “More Intelligence for a Brighter Tomorrow.” Green alien: “But what would they do with trillions of money?” Blue alien: “Build more intelligence.” Green alien: “Ah! So the money is merely an intermediate mechanism. They charge for the intelligence to fund the next generation of intelligence, ultimately making it universally available.”
9. A trader watches stock charts beside a whiteboard reading “Higher usage, more data, more revenue, bigger models, more money, repeat” and a mug reading “Profits Forever.” Blue alien: “They also use it to make more money.” Green alien: “Why?” Blue alien: “Because they like money.”
10. A champagne party in front of a towering pile of cash under a banner reading “More Money Forever.” Green alien: “Yes, but surely at some point—” Blue alien: “They really like money.”
11. A tycoon in sunglasses swims in a sea of banknotes amid fireworks, yachts and champagne, beside a sign reading “They really like money.” The aliens watch silently.
